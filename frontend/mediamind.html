    🔄 Convert Media

    Run FFmpeg-powered operations on files already in MinIO. Results download to your browser.

    🎬 Convert Format

    Enter the exact blob name returned by the Upload page. The converted file downloads to your browser and is saved to MinIO's converted bucket.

    ⏳ Conversion runs server-side. Large files may take several minutes. Don't close this tab.
    🌊 Waveform Visualizer

    Generates an MP4 video showing the audio waveform. Optionally overlay a background image. The result downloads to your browser and is saved to MinIO.

    No image selected
    💬 Auto-Caption Video

    Runs Whisper transcription on your video, generates an SRT subtitle file, then burns the captions directly into the video using FFmpeg. The captioned MP4 downloads to your browser and is saved to MinIO.

    ⏳ This runs Whisper + FFmpeg in sequence. Expect 2–5× the video duration for processing time.
    📝 Transcribe Audio / Video

    Uses OpenAI Whisper (base model) to convert speech to text. A .txt file downloads to your browser and is saved to MinIO.

    ⬇️ Download File
